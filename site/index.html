FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

Do you travel outside Bangalore?

Yes, we undertake projects across India.

What drone do you use?

We use custom-built FPV drones and cinematic camera systems.

Can you shoot indoors?

Yes, provided the environment is safe and permissions are in place.

How far in advance should I book?

Ideally 2–3 weeks in advance so we can plan permissions, flight paths, and rehearsals — but we can accommodate faster timelines for urgent projects.

Can you work alongside our existing production crew?

Absolutely — we regularly integrate with directors, DOPs, and agencies, and plan our flight paths around your shot list.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted individually based on location, complexity, and deliverables. Send us a brief through the contact form and we'll respond within 24 hours.
